# Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics Dashboard
### RMX Industries | Looker Studio | Multi-Account BI

---

## Overview

Built a fully dynamic, corporate-level Business Intelligence dashboard in Google Looker Studio to drive DI account growth, forecasting accuracy, and margin optimization across **250+ customer accounts** spanning USA, Canada, and ROW regions.

This replaced top-down assumption-based forecasting with true account-level run-rate analysis — giving the sales and management team a single source of truth for revenue, margin, and category performance.

---

## Problem Statement

- No unified view across 250+ customer accounts
- Sales forecasting relied on top-down assumptions — not actual account velocity
- Margin compression and at-risk accounts were invisible until revenue was already missed
- No way to identify whitespace expansion opportunities across product categories

---

## Solution Built

A fully refreshable, multi-layered Looker Studio dashboard with zero manual updates required after monthly data pull.

### Dashboard Layers

| Layer | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| Corporate Overview | Total revenue, GM%, category mix across all accounts |
| Account-Level Drill | Per-account revenue, margin, SKU performance |
| 3-Year YTD Comparison | FY24 vs FY25 vs FY26 side-by-side |
| TTM Run-Rate | Last 90 Days vs Trailing 12 Month monthly analysis |
| Risk Flag System | Automated alerts for declining accounts |
| Whitespace Analysis | Category penetration gaps across 11 product categories |

---

## Key Features

- **3-Year YTD Revenue Comparison** — FY24 vs FY25 vs FY26 using fully dynamic calculated fields
- **TTM Run-Rate Analysis** — Last 90 Days vs Trailing 12 Month to detect early revenue signals
- **Automated Flag System** — identifies declining SKUs, margin compression, single-category concentration risk, and below-threshold accounts
- **Top 10 Declining SKU Analysis** — YoY% and 2-year % change tracking
- **Category Penetration Whitespace** — expansion opportunities across 11 product categories
- **Margin Health Monitoring** — account-level GM% vs company average benchmark
- **Top 5 Movers Panel** — growing SKUs, declining SKUs, margin leak identification
- **Fully Refreshable** — same data pull process every month, zero rework

---

## Key Learnings

- TTM run-rate is significantly more reliable than YTD-only comparisons for mid-year forecasting
- Single-category accounts represent the highest-value, lowest-effort expansion opportunity
- Margin compression is almost always visible 60–90 days before it shows up in P&L reviews
- A dashboard that refreshes from the same data pull every month gets used — one that requires manual prep doesn't
